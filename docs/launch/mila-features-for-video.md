# Mila: everything that works (copy and paste into the launch video)

Every prompt below was run against Mila on Oct 23 and gave the answer shown. Type them in this order for a clean demo. Nothing sends, posts or deletes without the agent's OK.

## One-line pitch
**Mila is the AI operations manager for real estate agents.** Tell her what's happening in plain English. She keeps your contacts, calendar, listings, follow-ups and posts in order, so you can sell.

## The demo script (in order, about 2 minutes)
1. `Add buyer Dana Whitfield 301-555-0188 budget 550k looking in Rockville`
   → Contact saved with phone, budget and area.
2. `Showing at 14 Elm Street, Rockville MD tomorrow at 2pm`
   → On your calendar, with "Remind me 1 hour before" and "Start showing sheet".
3. `Set up an open house at 9 Pine Court, Bethesda MD Saturday 1 to 4`
   → Open house booked, with checklist, reminders and a post ready.
4. `Remind me Thursday at 9 to send comps to Dana`
   → Reminder saved, every word kept.
5. `Add 22 Birch Lane, Gaithersburg MD 20877, $625,000, 4 bed 3 bath 2400 sqft`
   → Listing saved from one sentence.
6. `Make a Just Listed post for 22 Birch Lane`
   → Instagram carousel with your photos, price strip and agent card. Edit, restyle, save as images.
7. `Draft an email to Dana thanking her for the showing`
   → Email draft in your voice. Nothing sends until you do.
8. `Text Dana confirming the showing`
   → Text ready; one tap opens it in Messages.
9. `Offer accepted on 22 Birch Lane at $610,000 closing December 15`
   → Whole deal timeline on your calendar and to-dos: earnest money, inspection, appraisal, financing, walkthrough, closing.
10. `I will be out of town next Wednesday through Friday`
    → Time off blocked, conflicts flagged, reminders the evening before.
11. `Summarize Dana`
    → Who she is, where she stands, and Do now / Coming up / Later steps.
12. `Who should I follow up with?`
    → Your priority list for today.

## Money and numbers (instant)
- `payment 615k 20% 6.75` → principal and interest $3,191/month, plus taxes and insurance estimate
- `seller gross 479k 5.5%` → commission $26,345 and a full net sheet
- `commission on 479k at 2.5%` → $11,975
- `reduction percentage from $689,900 to $674,900` → $15,000, about 2.17%
- `which of my buyers are under $600k?` → only the buyers who are

## Advice agents ask for
- `Explain the 45 and 180 day rules for a 1031 exchange`
- `What are the MD transfer and recordation taxes?`
- `What documents do I need to list a home in Maryland?`
- `Is $675,000 a reasonable offer on a home listed at $689,000?`
- `My listing has been sitting 60 days. How should I handle the price?`
  (Each ends with a "check with your broker / attorney / CPA" note, and never invents local numbers.)

## Features to show on screen
- **Chat** that does the work, plus voice input.
- **Calendar**: events, conflicts, time off; add to Google Calendar, or Apple Calendar via a private feed; "Add to my calendar" on every event.
- **Contacts**: pipeline stages, messages and follow-up tabs, import from Google Sheets with a review screen, client summary card on each page.
- **Properties**: past, current and upcoming, with photos and facts you saved.
- **Posts**: Just Listed, Coming Soon, Open House, Just Sold, anniversary; carousel of up to 10 photos; Full photo design plus 8 other styles; save as 1080x1350 images.
- **To-dos**: drag to sort; overdue ones become "Catch up".
- **Approvals**: Mila drafts and asks; she never sends, posts or deletes on her own.
- **Out-of-office** button on Calendar.
- **Invite & earn** referral page.
- **Plans**: 7-day free trial, no card. Mila $39/mo (1,200 credits). Annual $390/yr (two months free). Team: custom.
- **Sign in** with Google.

## Safe to say in the video
- "Mila never sends, posts or deletes without your OK."
- "Your calendar stays yours: Mila adds events, she never reads yours."
- "Fair Housing built in."

## Do NOT show or claim
- Gmail or Outlook (not offered), MLS data ("coming soon"), pulling photos from Zillow/Redfin, designed PDF flyers (copy only), reading the agent's inbox.
- Order matters: use your own photos on posts for the best-looking result.
