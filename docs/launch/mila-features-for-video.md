# Mila: working features

## Chat (type it like you'd text a coworker)
- Add contacts: buyer, seller, renter, lead; phone, email, budget, area, notes in one sentence
- Households ("Hollis and Mae Brandt"), duplicate detection, new-lead phrasing ("new lead from Zillow, Priya Shah…")
- Look up anything saved: phone, email, budget, "tell me about Dana", "which buyers are under $600k?"
- Notes and facts saved to the right client or home
- Voice input

## Calendar
- Showings, open houses, meetings, lunch, appointments from plain English ("tomorrow at 2pm", "Saturday 1 to 4")
- Conflict warnings with Keep / Move / Find another time
- Move, reschedule, cancel (always asks to confirm)
- Time off / out of office with conflicts and pre-departure reminders
- "Block off Friday afternoon" holds the hours
- Licence-state warning for out-of-state showings
- Google Calendar connect (Mila adds events), Apple/any calendar via private feed, "Add to my calendar" on every event
- Week and day overview, free-time check

## Reminders and to-dos
- "Remind me Thursday at 9 to send comps to Dana"
- Tasks, drag to sort, overdue become "Catch up" items
- Mark done, list, cancel reminders
- Long pasted notes split into separate actions (descriptive lines are only noted)

## Properties and deals
- Add a listing from one sentence (address, price, beds, baths, sqft)
- Status: coming soon, active, under contract, sold; list date
- Log offers, offer accepted builds the full transaction timeline (earnest money, inspection, appraisal, financing, walkthrough, closing)
- Property notes, turnout logging, listing description, flyer copy, prep-for-address
- My Properties: past, current, upcoming, with photos

## Open houses
- Book, sign-in sheet, checklist, reminders, follow-ups for visitors, recap

## Posts and images
- Just Listed, Coming Soon, Open House, price drop, Just Sold, anniversary
- Instagram carousel up to 10 images, per-image text or no text
- Full-photo design plus 8 other styles, agent card last slide
- Your own photos, remove, reorder; saves as 1080x1350 images
- Another version, rewrite, change headline, Fair Housing checks

## Messages (drafts only, nothing sends itself)
- Email and text drafts in your voice, correct first name, convert email to text
- Opens in your email app / Messages, or copy
- Follow-up batches after showings

## Client intelligence
- "Summarize Dana": who they are, where they stand
- Do now / Coming up / Later steps by client type (buyer, seller, renter, past client)
- Summary card on each contact page
- Priorities: who to follow up with today

## Numbers
- Payment (P&I, taxes, insurance estimate), PITI, net sheet, commission, reduction percent, net price with credits, budget compare

## Advice
- 1031 timing, MD transfer and recordation tax, listing documents, appraisal gap options, offer reasonableness, stale-listing pricing, sharing client info, hottest lead
- Always ends with a check-with-your-broker/attorney/CPA note, never invents local figures

## Safety
- Fair Housing refusals, no portal scraping, prompt-injection refusal, confirm before delete, nothing sent/posted/deleted without OK

## App
- 7-day free trial, no card; Mila $39/mo (1,200 credits); Annual $390/yr; Team custom
- Google sign-in; Contacts import from Google Sheets with review screen
- Invite & earn referrals; support tickets inside the app
- Credits history with every charge and grant
- Mobile app feel, light/dark

## Not offered
Gmail/Outlook, MLS data (coming soon), Zillow/Redfin photos, designed PDF flyers, inbox reading.
