# Video playbook

**Format:** 1080x1080 (or 1080x1350 for more feed space), 20s, 60fps, new song each time, beat-synced. End card: wordmark, tagline, **Comment "Mila" for a 7-day free trial**. No URL in the video yet.

| # | Market | Style | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Malibu | White, clean, glassy. Typing, phone, expands into a photo grid, app tour | Best performer in the owner's eyes. The benchmark. |
| 2 | New York | Day-to-night, sun/moon arc, editorial | Felt weaker than #1 |
| 3 | Texas | Bold Americana, stamps, "Contract to Keys" | Felt weaker than #1 |
| 4 | Maryland | Chaos to calm (see video-prompts/04-maryland.md) | Built to convert |

**What made #1 work (keep this):** one human moment (someone typing a plain sentence), one signature transition, clean and uncluttered, every cut on a beat, and the app looks real.
**What to avoid:** too many ideas per video, concept over clarity, fake-looking app screens.

**Conversion rules**
- Hook in the first 1 second (a line on screen that creates curiosity or tension).
- Captions on screen: most people watch muted.
- One call to action only.
- Never present made-up numbers as real customer results. Label demo data.
- Post natively, not links. Pin the "Comment MILA" comment.

**Comment-to-trial funnel:** comment "Mila" > reply and DM the link > they start the 7-day trial. Right now the owner replies by hand. Link in bio should go to the waitlist site once it exists.

**Always attach to the video tool:** logo files, app screenshots (launch-kit/), the property photos, the song.
