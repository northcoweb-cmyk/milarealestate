# Vision and positioning

**One line:** Your AI operations manager for real estate.
**Not:** a generic "AI productivity app".

**What it does:** the agent says what they need in plain English. Mila does the work (listing prep, posts, emails, texts, open houses, showings, follow-ups, meeting prep, calendar, deal checklist) and asks before anything goes out. Everything waits in "Ready for approval".

**Who it's for:** US real-estate agents, solo first, then teams and brokerages.

**The investor test (from feedback):** would a $100M agent trust it to run their ops? Four demo tests that must always work:
1. Open house with no address given ("set me up for my open house")
2. Meeting prep
3. Showing follow-ups
4. "What am I forgetting today?"

**Principles**
- Mila never sends or deletes without the agent's OK.
- No vendor or backend names anywhere a user can see.
- No fake features: unconnected things say Connect / Coming soon. Unverified property facts are never used.
- Mobile-first, feels like a real native app, smooth, no glitches.
- The owner's NorthCo business always comes first over any other plan or idea.

**Voice:** calm, specific, in charge of the busywork. Short sentences. Always a clear next step. No hype.

**Roadmap idea from the investor:** Mila progressively learns how each agent operates (their style, timing, templates), so it gets better the longer it's used. This is the moat.

## Brand and UI direction
Use the website's purples and cool blues inside the app too (soft blue to violet to peach gradient, white cards, Instrument Serif for headlines, Inter for text). The goal is one continuous look from the waitlist site to the app, so nothing feels like a new product after signing up.
