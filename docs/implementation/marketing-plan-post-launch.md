# Mila marketing plan, in order (saved Oct 22; launch Fri Oct 23, 10 AM ET)

Priority rule: NorthCo business first. Spend nothing on ads until the product proves it keeps people.

## Phase 0: launch day (Oct 23). Soft launch to the real waitlist (1 to 2 people, up to 25 seats)
- Send the launch email from Mila OS (manual SEND). Personally message each waitlist person too, and sit with them through first setup if they will let you.
- Goal: each person finishes setup (name, brokerage, one contact, one showing) and uses Mila 3 days in a row.
- Watch Mila OS Errors and Feedback all day. Fix only blockers. One push per batch.

## Phase 1: days 1 to 7. Prove it works for real people
1. Write down every question Mila fumbled (Feedback → "Copy today's reports for Claude"); each becomes a regression test first.
2. Day-3 check-in message to every active user: "What did Mila get wrong? What did you wish she did?"
3. Track: sign-ups, finished setup, day-1 activation (added a contact AND a showing), day-3 return, credits used per user, AI cost per user, errors per day.
4. Targets before spending anything: 60% finish setup, 40% return on day 3, AI cost under $11.50 per paid user per month.

## Phase 2: weeks 1 to 3. Founder-led outreach (free)
1. Message agents you know personally (Keller Williams Gaithersburg, your partner agent, past colleagues) with the 7-day free trial link. Ask each for two introductions.
2. Referral program is already built (200 credits to the referrer, 100 to the friend). Mention it in the day-3 email and on the Invite & earn page.
3. Join 2 or 3 local agent Facebook and Instagram groups as yourself; help first, mention Mila only when it answers a question someone asked.

## Phase 3: weeks 2 to 6. Content that shows the product (cheap)
1. Daily 20 to 30 second screen recordings of real tasks: "open house set up in 20 seconds", "client summary in one tap", "post from your own photos". Post to Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
2. Sarah makes UGC: an agent day-in-the-life using Mila. 3 per week.
3. One clear call to action everywhere: the 7-day free trial, no card.
4. Reuse the best clip as the top of the waitlist site.

## Phase 4: weeks 4 to 8. Referral and affiliate engine
1. Affiliates: agents with audiences get a code and a share of what their sign-ups pay; start with 5 hand-picked people.
2. Case study from the first 3 to 5 happy users (a short quote and a number: hours saved a week).
3. Annual plan push to happy users ($390/year, two months free, +500 bonus credits): it improves cash and retention.

## Phase 5: months 2 to 4. Teams and brokerages (the big lever toward $100k MRR)
1. One brokerage or team sale is 10 to 50 seats. Pitch after you have 5 happy users and a case study.
2. Offer a pilot: one team, 30 days, a shared pipeline and branded look (Team plan, custom price).
3. Ask each pilot team lead for an introduction to two other team leads.

## Phase 6: months 3 to 6. Search and AI-search presence
1. SEO pages for real questions agents search ("open house checklist", "how to write a just listed post", "1031 exchange timeline"), each ending in a try-Mila link.
2. Keep llms.txt and the waitlist knowledge base current so AI assistants describe Mila correctly.
3. Google verification approved (removes the "unverified app" screen), then reopen outreach to cold audiences.

## Phase 7: later
- Paid ads only once activation and day-3 return hit the Phase 1 targets and the payback on a $39 plan is under 3 months.
- Licensed property data (RentCast already in; Bright MLS vendor access with your partner agent), then the physical Mila product.

## Money guardrails (start small)
- Google Cloud billing on with a $20 to 25 monthly budget alert; OpenAI $20 to 25 loaded with a hard monthly limit about $30; photo provider off until agents ask for it. Raise limits when paid users pass 10.
