# Growth plan: own the "Mila" search results and build toward $100k MRR

## 1. What "top result" realistically means
- "Mila" alone is a generic name (other products and an AI research institute use it). Nobody can promise #1 for it. The winnable targets, in order: **"Mila Real Estate"**, **"Mila AI real estate"**, **"Mila AI for real estate agents"**, then **"AI assistant for real estate agents"** (competitive, long game).
- Google ranks things it understands as one entity. So everything must say the same thing: name "Mila" / "Mila Real Estate", one-line description, same logo, same site link, on every profile.

## 2. Built on the site (shipped in the waitlist project)
- Title and description built around "Mila Real Estate", canonical URL, keywords.
- Structured data for Organization, WebSite, SoftwareApplication (with the $49 offer) and FAQPage (the FAQ is now one shared source).
- `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` that explicitly welcomes search crawlers and AI answer-engine crawlers (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot).
- `llms.txt`: a plain-language fact sheet for AI assistants, including "not to be confused with" so they don't mix Mila up with other products.
- `/guides` hub with four useful agent guides (open house checklist, listing launch checklist, AI for agents, daily workflow), each with Article markup. More guides = more ways to be found. Aim for 2 new guides a week after launch.

## 4. Checking whether it works (weekly, 10 minutes, I can log it)
Search "Mila Real Estate", "Mila AI real estate agents", "best AI assistant for real estate agents" in Google and Bing; ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude the same. Record: rank / whether Mila is named. Search Console shows impressions and clicks.

## 5. $100k MRR math (the assumptions are guesses until we have launch data)
- $100k/month = about 2,040 subscribers at $49, or a mix such as 1,200 solo agents ($58.8k) + 140 teams at $299 ($41.9k).
- If 25–40% of trials convert (an assumption, not data), that is about 5,000–8,000 trials. The US has roughly 1.5 million agents, so 2,000 paying is ~0.14% of them.
- Biggest levers, in order: (1) **brokerage and team deals** (one broker sale = 10–50 seats; the Serhant S.MPLE insight: everyone but their agents is unserved), (2) **referral program** (a free month or credits per paid referral), (3) **UGC and short video** (Sarah's TikTok/IG), (4) **coaches and influencers on affiliate** (20–30% revenue share for 12 months), (5) **SEO and AI-answer presence** (slow, compounding), (6) **annual plan** (cash up front, lower churn).
- Pricing options to test after two weeks of real cost data: keep $49 solo; add annual (~2 months free); a higher tier for teams ($299 now) and a custom brokerage tier. Don't cut the price before we know cost per user.

## 6. Product moves that grow it
Deeper integrations (Google/Microsoft calendar add-only, then email, then CRM imports like Follow Up Boss/CSV, MLS-feed partners), a referral screen in the app, a shareable "what Mila did this week" card, team workspaces, and a mobile-first onboarding that gets a first win in under 2 minutes. A physical Mila product is a later bet: validate demand with the software first.
