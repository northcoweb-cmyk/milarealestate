# Mila marketing playbook (Oct 22). Channels: X, TikTok (boosted), Instagram. Next: partners and teams.

## What the numbers already say
- One boosted TikTok: a couple thousand views, about 8 followers. That is roughly 0.4% view-to-follow. Fine for a first test, but followers are not customers. The goal of every boost from here is a **free-trial sign-up**, not a follow.
- Instagram 17 followers, posting daily and replying: right habit. Keep it; it builds the proof people check before they sign up.
- X: use it for credibility and building in public, not volume.

## The one message everywhere
"Mila is your AI operations manager. Tell her what you need in plain English and she handles the calendar, follow-ups, posts and open houses. 7-day free trial, no card."
Pick ONE proof per post: a task done in under 30 seconds on screen.

## Content engine (what to post, every day)
Rotate five formats. Each is a 15 to 30 second screen recording of the real app with big captions:
1. **"Watch me set up an open house in 20 seconds."** Type the request, show the calendar event, the post and the follow-up draft appearing.
2. **"Client summary in one tap."** Open a client: summary, do now, coming up, later. Hook: "Who should I call today?"
3. **"Post from your own photos."** Upload 3 photos, full-photo design, edit text, save. Hook: "Your listing photos deserve better than a Canva template."
4. **"Mila vs my Monday."** Voice-note a messy list ("showing at 2, call Priya, remind me to send comps"); Mila sorts it.
5. **"Questions agents ask"** (1031 timeline, appraisal gap, can I share the sale price with her son). Mila answers with the broker-check note. Pure value, saves and shares.
Hook formula for the first 2 seconds: a real agent pain ("I forgot to follow up again"), a number ("20 seconds"), or a mistake ("Stop doing open houses like this").
Always end with: "7-day free trial, link in bio."

## Boosting TikTok (do more, but smartly)
1. **Boost only what already works.** Post organically first. If a video gets 1,000+ views in its first 6 to 24 hours or strong saves/shares, boost it. Do not boost a dud.
2. **Objective:** website visits / link clicks (not followers, not video views). Send to the waitlist or sign-up page.
3. **Audience:** United States, age 25 to 55, interests real estate, house hunting, entrepreneurship, sales. Start broad on location, not narrow.
4. **Budget:** $5 to $10 a day per video for 3 days. Cap total at $50 to $75 until a video produces trial sign-ups. Scale only the winner.
5. **Track it:** every post gets its own link (add `?ref=tt1`, `?ref=tt2`...). Count sign-ups per ref. If you cannot see sign-ups by source yet, ask me and I will add a "where did you hear about Mila" field to sign-up.
6. **Kill rule:** after $20 with zero trial sign-ups, stop that video and change the hook, not the budget.

## Instagram and X
- IG: Reels daily (same videos), a Story each day with one question to the audience, reply to every comment within an hour, DM every follower a short welcome ("thanks for the follow, want to try Mila free?"). Pin the best Reel.
- X: one build-in-public post a day (what shipped, what broke, a real number), plus reply to agents and brokerages. Link in bio, not in every post.
- Cross-post the same clip to Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Change the caption, not the video.

## Becoming the top pick for agents
1. **Proof beats promises.** Case study from the first 3 to 5 users: one quote and one number (hours saved). Put it on the site.
2. **Be where agents already ask.** Local agent groups, brokerage Facebook groups, real estate subreddits: answer questions with real help; mention Mila only when it fits.
3. **Own the questions.** Short pages and posts for the exact things agents search ("open house checklist", "just listed caption", "1031 exchange timeline"), each ending in the trial. This also feeds AI assistants (llms.txt is already set).
4. **Referral loop** (built): 200 credits for the referrer, 100 for the friend. Remind every happy user at day 3.
5. **Reviews:** after a user's first good week, ask for a short testimonial and permission to use it.

## Partnering with teams and brokerages (your idea: share the subscription revenue)
Margins: a $39 plan has about $11.50 of AI cost ceiling and about $1.50 in card fees, leaving roughly $26 a month. A 20% share ($7.80) still leaves about $18.
Recommended terms to offer (draft, you have no lawyer: use a free agreement generator and have one real person read it later):
- **Individual affiliate / agent partner:** 20% of what each referred agent pays, for 12 months, paid monthly. Applies to monthly and annual ($78 on a $390 annual plan, paid after the 30-day refund window).
- **Team or brokerage partner:** 25% for 12 months on every agent they bring, plus a branded look and one shared pipeline for the team. Custom price for 10+ seats.
- Rules: paid only on money actually collected; no payment on refunds or chargebacks; self-referral not allowed; partner must not make false claims (no "guaranteed leads").
- Start with 3 to 5 hand-picked partners, not an open program. Give each a unique link and code so every sign-up is attributed.
- **What I need to build:** partner links and attribution on sign-up, a partner dashboard in Mila OS (sign-ups, active, owed), and a monthly payout CSV. Tell me when you want it and I will build it after launch week.
